Casino Royale

Project details

Casino Royale Gala 2026: development of the visual identity for my school's 2026 gala, organized by the student union (BDE). Based on the Casino Royale movie theme, the identity was applied across a range of communication materials, including posters, promotional visuals, website graphics, and Instagram stories. The project also included the design and production of printed materials used as decorative elements throughout the gala venue.
